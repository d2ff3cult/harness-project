# Intent Card Template

## 1. Business Goal
- What outcome should this page/flow deliver?

## 2. Actors
- Who triggers the behavior?
- Who is restricted?

## 3. Events And Decisions
- Trigger events
- Confirmation dialogs
- Confirm branch behavior
- Cancel branch behavior

## 4. Data Contracts
- Read APIs
- Write APIs
- Field-level mapping notes

## 5. Risk And Unknowns
- Risk points
- Unknown decisions that must not be guessed

## 6. Acceptance Signals
- What must be true to pass?
